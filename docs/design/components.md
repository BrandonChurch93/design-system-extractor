# App components

Every component of the app's own interface. Each has anatomy, states, responsive behavior, tokens, and motion. All of them render in `/catalog` from sample data. Component names here are the component file names in `apps/web/components/` (PascalCase) and the names used in `docs/design/pages.md`.

Shared rules: every interactive element is a real `button`, `a[href]`, `input` or `label`; every icon-only control has `aria-label`; every component respects reduced motion; every component works at 390 px; nothing is available only on hover (anything a tooltip would show is shown by a Popover that opens on click and on focus, and the same information exists elsewhere on the page).

Type styles and sizes named below are the ones in `docs/design/design-system.md`. No other sizes exist.

## Primitives

**TopNav** · Wordmark "Design System Extractor" (`h3` weight 700) on the left, links on the right: Showcase, Recent runs, How it works, GitHub. Height 68, `line` rule below drawn on load. Phone: wordmark plus a menu button (Lucide `menu`) that opens a disclosure list under the bar, slide from top, `motion-base`. Not sticky; the run page's SectionNav is the sticky element.

**Footer** · Two lines on `bg` above a `line` rule: left, "Open source · MIT · Built by Brandon Church" (link to his portfolio), "About · Privacy · Terms", and "Built with" linking `/catalog`, all `caption`; right, context text the page supplies (a share link, a cost line). Phone: stacks.

**SectionNav** · The run page's section links: Tokens, Motion, Components, Checks, Drift, Exports. Pills (`radius-pill`), `label` weight 500; the active section has `ink` ground and `inverse-ink` text. Sticky at top 0 once the header scrolls past, on `bg` with a `line` rule beneath that draws in when it sticks. Active state follows scroll position (IntersectionObserver). Phone: horizontally scrollable, no wrap, 20 px edge fade.

**Button** · Variants `primary` (`ink` ground, `inverse-ink` text), `ghost` (`surface` ground, `line` border, `line-strong` on hover), `inverse` (on `inverse-bg`: `inverse-ink` ground, `ink` text). Sizes `md` 44 high `space-5` side padding, `sm` 36 high `space-4`, `xl` 56 high `space-6` (the Extract button only). Optional leading Lucide icon 16. States: hover, focus-visible, active (scale 0.985), disabled (50% opacity, no pointer). Never full width except, on phones, the Extract button and the buttons of the state pages' forms (D-059).

**Chip** · A filter pill: 32 high, `label`, `line` border, `surface` ground. Selected: `ink` ground, `inverse-ink` text. Size `sm` (24 high, `caption` weight 600, `space-2` side padding) for a ChipGroup that sits on a preview, the card's Ground toggle (D-110). A count may follow the label in `meta`: "Buttons · 6". A ChipGroup is a single-select radio group with arrow-key movement. Phone: the row scrolls horizontally.

**Tag** · A token or category chip: 24 high, `tag` mono, `surface-tint` ground, `radius-pill`. Static text, except inside the component panel where a Tag is a button that opens the token's Popover.

**Pill** · A status marker: 24 high, `caption` weight 600, `radius-pill`, transparent ground, 1 px border. Variants: `solid` (`ink` border and text: Complete, New) and `soft` (`line-strong` border, `muted` text: Changed, Removed, Blocked, Running, Failed, Cancelled, Deterministic). A Pill with a filled ground (`ink` ground, `inverse-ink` text) is the `inverse` variant, used for "Model · Haiku" and "Model · Sonnet". The `soft` variant also carries `Named by the site` on a token that took the site's own name (D-082) and the kind of a motion pattern.

**Card** · `surface`, `line-soft` border, `radius-lg`, `shadow-card`. Padding `space-5` (`space-4` phone). A Card that is a link lifts on hover and carries the focus ring. Cards never nest more than one level.

**Input** · Sizes `md` 44 high and `xl` 56 high (the URL field in the home headline block only). `surface` ground, `line` border, `radius-md`, `body` text, `muted` placeholder. Hover `line-strong`, focus ring. Always paired with a `label` element, visually hidden where the design shows none. Error: a `caption` line below in `ink-2` with a Lucide `circle-alert` icon; the border does not change color (there is no red).

**Tabs** · A row of text tabs for switching content in place (the Code tabs in the panel, the compare mode is a separate control): `label` weight 500, `ink-2`, 2 px underline in `ink` on the active tab that slides between tabs over `motion-base`. `role="tablist"` with arrow-key movement; panels are `role="tabpanel"`.

**Popover** · A floating Card (`shadow-card`, max width as the caller says) anchored to its trigger with 8 px offset, flipping to stay in the viewport. Opens on click and on Enter or Space; closes on Escape, on outside click, and when focus leaves; focus moves into it on open and returns to the trigger on close. Entrance: slide 8 px from the anchor side plus opacity, `motion-fast`. On phones, a Popover wider than 320 px opens as a Sheet instead. Used by KeyPopover, PageListPopover, TokenPopover, SharePopover, StatesStrip, CheckBadge, SpacingBar.

**Sheet** · A bottom panel for phones and for large popovers: rises from the bottom to the height the caller says (92% for the component sheet, content height otherwise, max 80%), `surface`, `radius-lg` top corners, `shadow-sheet`, a 36 × 4 grab handle in `line-strong` with an `aria-label`, the `scrim` behind it (never blurred). Closes on the handle drag, Escape, scrim click. Focus trapped inside; returns to the trigger on close. Slide entrance, `motion-slow`.

**Toast** · Bottom right, `inverse-bg` ground, `inverse-ink` text, `radius-md`, slide in from bottom, auto-dismiss 4 s, pause on hover, one at a time, `role="status"`. Used for "Copied", "Link copied", and the mock sign-in message.

**Skeleton** · `skeleton` on `bg`, `surface-tint` on `surface`, `radius-sm`, exact height of the content it stands for. No shimmer; skeletons are still. They leave by shrinking (extend reverse).

**PreviewFrame** · The sandboxed iframe that shows a live preview: `sandbox=""`, `src` on the storage origin (or the local `dse serve` base), `title="{component name} preview"` (or `"{name} · {state} preview"`), sized to the preview's recorded width and height, `loading="lazy"` below the fold. A captured preview uses an `img` with the crop and the same alt text instead. The frame's region is painted with the preview's `ground` (live and captured alike), so a dark site's button sits on its own dark ground with no halo (D-094). On a card, a preview larger than its region is scaled down to fit, on width and on height, never up and never below 0.6× (`docs/specs/preview-fidelity.md`, Sizing; D-107): one that does not fit at 0.6× renders at 0.6× anchored to the region's top-left at a `space-4` inset and is clipped at the region's edge, with a 40 px fade from transparent into the painted ground on each clipped edge. The scale caption from copy (`Shown at 0.8×`, or `Shown at 0.6×, cut to fit` when clipped) sits on a card in the control strip under the preview region (ComponentCard; D-112), and elsewhere inside the ground at its bottom-left. The frame takes the pointer (no `pointer-events: none`), so the site's hover and active styles play inside it on the card and in the drawer (D-106); it keeps `tabindex="-1"`, so the page's tab order does not grow by every component's inner controls; keyboard users reach the focus state through the StateBar in the drawer, and a pointer click inside the frame focuses the element (`:focus-visible` shows only for keyboard focus, as on the site). In the drawer the preview renders at 1:1 and the stage scrolls horizontally when the preview is wider than it (a scrollbar in the app's `line` color, no caption). When the contrast fallback applies (`preview-fidelity.md`, Preview ground; D-109) the region is painted with the opposite tone, and the ground caption from copy shows under the drawer's stage and in the card's control strip while the toggle is on that tone (D-112). A captured preview with reason `hidden-until-focus` shows its focus crop with the hidden caption from copy, and one with reason `no-crop` shows the component's markup in a CodeBlock (the drawer's Code section's HTML) with the no-capture caption, never an image request (D-108). Those two captions sit at the region's top-left, wrapping, on a `surface` note with a `line-soft` border and `radius-sm`, leaving the `Captured` tag its top-right corner (D-111, D-112). In the panel it gains, above the states list: a StateBar, and a Ground toggle (a three-Chip ChipGroup labeled `Ground`: `Site` the preview's ground, the default; `Light` the `surface` token; `Dark` the `inverse-bg` token), for reading a light component against a dark ground and the reverse; and the font caption from copy when the preview's `fontsSubstituted` is set.

**StateBar** · A ChipGroup of state Chips (the State labels, only the states captured for the component, in the State enum order, `Default` first and selected) under the landmark label `States`. For a live preview, selecting a state swaps the frame's `src` to that state's file (`preview-{state}.html`), which plays the state's transition on load as the site plays it; selecting it again reloads and replays it. For a captured preview it switches between the state crops. When the component has `motion.transitions`, a `sm` ghost Button `Play` (Lucide `play`) follows the chips: it steps Default → Hover → Active → Default by swapping files, each state held for its longest recorded duration plus 400 ms (600 ms at least), once; states the component does not have are skipped. Under reduced motion `Play` still steps the states but each file's animation is not played (the frame shows the end values; the files carry their own reduced-motion rule). Phone: the chips scroll horizontally, `Play` stays at the end of the row. Catalog entries in every state at desktop and 390.

**CaptureViewer** · A full-screen lightbox (a Sheet on phones) showing one page capture image at its viewport width, scrolled so a given bounding box is in view, with that box outlined by a 2 px `ink` rectangle; a Close icon button; Escape closes; focus returns to the opener. `img` alt is "{path} at {viewport} px".

**Meter** · 8 px track `skeleton`, fill `ink`, `radius-pill`; fill extends. Used for the daily budget on the budget-reached state and the dev spend readout in `/catalog`.

**CodeBlock** · `surface-tint` ground, `line-soft` border, `radius-sm`, `code` text, horizontal scroll, a copy button top right (icon-only, `aria-label="Copy"`). Lines never wrap.

**LiveLog** · `inverse-bg` ground, `inverse-ink` text at `code`, timestamps in `inverse-muted`, `radius-lg`, max height 320 with internal scroll, collapsible with a disclosure in the header row; collapsed shows the last line only. Autoscroll only when already at the bottom.

**EmptyState** · Inside a Card: `h3` sentence, `body-sm` `muted` line, one Button. Used by Recent runs with no runs. A category chip on the home page with zero showcase cards is not rendered at all, so the showcase never needs an empty state.

**StateCard** · The blocked-state layout: a 640-wide Card centered with `space-7` padding: `label` eyebrow, `title-sm` title, `body` `muted` explanation, a detail region (CodeBlock, Meter, or a list), one primary action, secondary actions after a `line-soft` rule. Used by every `/state/*` route and the failed, cancelled and not-found run states.

## Motion (D-081)

Every motion primitive follows the Card, Chip, Pill and CodeBlock tokens. Curves are a 1.5 px `ink` stroke on a `surface-tint` ground with a `line` baseline; a replay track is a `line` hairline and its dot is 8 px `ink`. Every one has a catalog entry in every state at desktop and 390.

**DurationStrip** · One horizontal scale on a base-10 log axis from the shortest duration to the longest (a single duration sits at the center), so 150, 1,000 and 10,000 ms are evenly spaced (D-102); every duration a tick: a 16 px (`space-4`) 1 px `ink` mark, the `hex` value (`{ms} ms`) above it, the role under it in `caption` `muted` when it has one (Faster, Fast, Base, Slow, Slower), the most used marked with a filled 8 px `ink` dot on the baseline. Each tick is a TokenPopover trigger, so a tick whose label is dropped still answers: a tick whose value label is dropped is always a trigger; a trigger's target is 24 px across, so of the other ticks, where they sit closer than that to a trigger, the most used take the triggers and the rest are drawn, not pressed (`pointer-events: none`; D-111, D-112). Labels stagger: value labels take at most two rows above the scale and role labels at most two rows below, assigned greedily by x position with a minimum of 56 px between labels on one row; a value label that fits on neither row is dropped (the tick, its dot and its popover stay), and the strip's meta line says `{n} durations · {k} labeled` when any is dropped; a tick counts as labeled only when its value and, if it has one, its role both show (D-112). The axis is inset by half the widest label, so no label leaves the strip's box at 390. Each tick carries a 4 px `ink` dot that rises 12 px up its mark over the duration's real length (`120 ms` runs 120 ms, linear, so the strip shows what fast means) on the EasingGrid's shared clock, and loops with the grid; a duration longer than the 1,400 ms travel runs for the travel and stops where it got to, and the strip never waits for it (D-093, D-097). The Long list: durations over 2,000 ms leave the scale and sit in a `Long` list to its right, the scale then ending at 2,000 ms; each row is a TokenPopover trigger in two columns, the name in `meta` (with its role after an interpunct when it has one) and the value in `hex` right-aligned (D-112). Adopted by the P18 measurement: on modernsoftworks.com the log scale alone dropped two value labels at 1440 (D-102, D-111). Phone: the scale keeps the full width; the Long list, when it exists, sits below the scale.

**EasingGrid** · EasingCards in a 3 / 2 / 1 grid, `space-5` gap, with the grid caption from copy beneath. Grid items stretch; the stage is what absorbs the difference between cards (D-101). It owns a shared clock (D-093): every card's animations are created with the same `startTime` on `document.timeline`, so they never drift apart. Each cycle runs the travel for 1,400 ms, holds at the end for 500 ms, fades the dots out over 150 ms, resets, fades them in over 150 ms and runs again, forever, while the section is in the viewport; an IntersectionObserver pauses every animation off screen and resumes them on the same clock. Because every curve runs the same distance in the same time, the differences between curves are what the eye catches. Under reduced motion no loop runs: each card shows the still curve with its dot drawn at 60% of the time and the track dot at the matching position, and the reduced-motion caption from copy replaces the grid caption.

**EasingCard** · A Card: the name (`h3`) with the `Named by the site` Pill when it applies, the value in `code` (wrapping at commas), the usage line in `caption` `muted`, always present (from the token's `usedBy`: `Buttons, cards and 2 scroll reveals`; `Declared, not seen in use` when `usedBy` is empty), then the EasingStage pinned to the card's bottom edge (`margin-top: auto` in the card's grid), so every stage in a row sits at the same height and every card in a row is the same height. Card padding is the Card's own (`space-5`, `space-4` phone), the stage inset by nothing more (D-101). No button: the loop is ambient (D-093).

**EasingStage** · A plot as wide as the card's inner width (the stage measures it with a ResizeObserver, as the DurationStrip does) and 160 px tall, holding the curve drawn as a 1.5 px `ink` stroke on a `surface-tint` ground with a `line` baseline and left edge, x for time and y for progress; the plot keeps room above and below for curves that overshoot, at least 15% of its height on each side. The dot on the curve is 8 px `ink`. Below the plot, at the same width, a `line` hairline track with a 10 px `ink` dot that travels left to right with the same easing; the track dot travels the full track width. Both dots keep the loop rules in `motion.md` (Ambient loops). The curve path is computed in pixels from the measured width, never stretched from a fixed viewBox, so the stroke stays 1.5 px (D-101). The track dot is one Web Animations API animation of `transform` with the token's value as the travel segment's `easing`; the curve dot is two, on two nested elements (x linear, y with the token's easing), because one animation would ease both axes and run the dot down the diagonal. So what the reader sees is the browser applying the real curve, not an approximation. `steps()` draws as a staircase and `linear()` as its stops joined.
