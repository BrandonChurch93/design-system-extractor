Why weekly

A week is long enough for a change to matter and short enough to remember why it happened.

People skim a list of ten changes. They read a note that explains three of them.

Tidewater groups merged pull requests by team, drops the ones marked internal, and asks the author for one sentence.

The digest goes out on Friday morning, before the weekend, while the work is still fresh.
